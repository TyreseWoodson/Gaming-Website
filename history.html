Legend of Zelda Lore

1986 - 1993

  • The franchise began in 1986 with the launch of “The Legend of Zelda” in Japan (The sequel being “The Adventure of Link”)
  • “A Link to the Past” was released as a prequel to the original legend of Zelda game with the main characters being the predecessors of the original link and zelda
  • “Links awakening” is a sequel to “A Link to the Past”
  • There were four releases of the game from 1986-1993
  • In order, the games go: “The Legend of Zelda”, “The Adventure of Link” (sequel to LOZ), “A Link to the Past” (prequel to LOZ), and “Links Awakening” (sequel to ALP)

1998 – 2001

  • In 1998, “Ocarina of Time” was released as a prequel to “A Link to the Past” in order to explain the Imprisoning War
  • In 2000, “Majora’s Mask” was released as a sequel of “Ocarina of Time” but takes place in a parallel world
  • In 2001, the LOZ timeline was reviewed and they later added entries for “Oracle of seasons” as well as “Oracle of Ages”

2002 – 2011

  • In 2002 and 2003, The Wind Waker was released as a distant future sequel to Ocarina of Time. It takes place in a time after the Great Flood washes away Hyrule. This game does not recognize the events that took place in “A Link to the Past”
  • In 2006, “Twilight Princess” was released as another sequel to Ocarina of Time, contradicting The Wind Waker. This is because there are two parallel timelines resulting from Links time travel in Ocarina of Time
  • From 2002 – 2005, “Four Swords”, “Four Swords Adventures,” and “The Minish Cap” were released. These games formed their own plotline
  • From 2007 – 2009, “Phantom Hourglass” and “Spirit Tracks” were made and released as sequels to “The Wind Waker”

2011 – Present

  • Shortly after “Skyward Sword” a full timeline was released in “Hyrule Historia”. This was to cover up any plot holes and resolve contradictions by adding a third timeline branch where Link is defeated by Ganon in “Ocarina of Time”
  • In “Creating a Champion”, the events that occurred in Hyrule’s history are described to have faded into myth
  • The timeline begins anew with “Breath of the Wild”
