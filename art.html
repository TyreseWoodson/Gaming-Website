Whether you're a seasoned hero or just beginning your quest, this guide will help you uncover the names, powers, and stories behind the iconic figures and legendary tools that shape the Zelda universe. Just click on the image you want to learn more about that character/item.


Iconic Characters

Din

The Goddess of Power, she is the creator of the earth, forging the land and its mountains, rivers, and structures. Her power is linked to strength and creation.

Farore

The Goddess of Courage, he is the one who created all living things. Farore represents growth, resilience, and the enduring spirit.

Nayru

The Goddess of Wisdom, she is credited with giving the world laws. Nayru is responsible for the creation of the laws of time and space.

Fi

She is the spirit that resides within the Goddess Sword and serves as Link's companion throughout the game.

Midna

Midna is a mischievous imp and the Twilight Princess. She becomes a loyal ally to Link, evolving into a selfless hero who sacrifices herself.

Daruk

Known for his immense strength and courageous spirit. He pilots the Divine Beast Vah Rudania and protects allies with his unique ability, Daruk's Protection.

Riju

Riju is the young chief of the Gerudo. She leads her people with courage and determination. Riju wields the power of Urbosa’s legacy.

Sidon

Prince of the Zoras in Breath of the Wild, Sidon is a courageous and optimistic leader who aids Link in reclaiming Divine Beast Vah Ruta.

Hylia

Goddess of Hyrule, after succumbing to her injuries during the war against Demise, she was reiencarnated into Zelda.

Zelda

reincarnation of the Goddess Hylia, linked in the Triforce as the Triforce of Wisdom. She has the power of healing and protection.

King of Hyrule

She is the spirit that resides within the Goddess Sword and serves as Link's companion throughout the game.

Revali

Known for his skill with the bow and his flight ability. He pilots Divine Beast Vah Medoh and often clashes with Link

Demise

Demon king who sought to conquer the world and obtain the Triforce. Possesses immense strength and magical abilities.

Ghirahim

Powerful demon in Skyward Sword who pursues Zelda to resurrect his master, Demise.

Ganondorf

King of the Gerudo tribe, consisting mostly of women with one male born every 100 years. He is the reincarnation of Demise's hatred

Dark Link

Dark version of Link mirroring his appearance, abilities, and fighting style. Never speaks or shows personality.

Bokoblin

Member of the demon king's army. These goblin-like creatures come in various colors and strengths, often attacking in groups with crude weapons.

Lynel

Member of the demon king's army. Known for their immense strength, speed, and deadly weaponry, they are among the toughest enemies Link can face.



Legendary Items

Master Sword

Created by Hylia, originally the mother sword, once it fell down to earth to await a new user, over time it became the master sword

Bows and Arrow

Long-range weapons that can come with a variety of enchantments like fire and ice

Hylian Shield

A large, heavy shield that bears the emblem of the Triforce.

Lens of Truth

A magical lens that allows the user to see through any illusion.

Boomerang

An airborne weapon that can be used to cut down or retrieve out-of-reach objects.

The Triforce

The Triforce consists of three pieces, each representing one of the goddesses: Power, Wisdom, and Courage.

Ocarina of Time

A magical musical instrument that can conjure different powers through song.

The Wind Waker

A magical conductor’s wand that invokes the powers of the wind.

Goddesses’ Harp

Said to be a divine instrument of the goddess who once watched over Hyrule.

Sheikah Slate

It serves as a map for Link and gives him access to different runes.

Rupee

The main form of currency in Hyrule.

Cucco

Chicken-like birds that, when held overhead, can allow one to briefly glide on air.

Potion

Restores health, stamina, and more, depending on the type.

Majora's Mask

Ancient and cursed mask capable of altering reality and bringing chaos. Manipulates others to fulfill its destructive desire.
